- **Text painted with `primary`, `brand`, `info`, `success` or `warning` now reads the derived `*-text` colour (#2885).** `.text-primary`, `.text-success`, `.text-warning`, `.text-info`, `.btn-link`, links, alert, toast, badge and status text and the alert/toast icons use `var(--primary-text, var(--primary))` and its siblings; backgrounds, borders and the status dots keep the fill (`-danger` included: that dot is restored to its pre-#3320 fill) (anything drawn from `currentColor` on a text element, such as an underline or an icon stroke, follows its text colour). The 347 `primary` / `info` / `success` / `warning` as-text pairs that were exempt in `A11Y_EXEMPTIONS` are now measured through the derived colours (the contrast matrix and `djust_theming.W001`) and pass with no exemption. Override the `-text` colour next to a fill you override while the theme generator is active.
