- **The VDOM bounds the nesting depth of the HTML it parses.** Every walker over the VDOM (diff, patch serialization, serialization back to HTML) recurses once per nesting level, so a page whose markup nests thousands of elements deep needs more stack than the rendering thread has. The parser now keeps at most `MAX_VDOM_DEPTH` (256) levels, counted from the LiveView root: an element at the limit keeps its tag and attributes, and everything below it is flattened to its text content, so nothing nests deeper and no text is lost (except below an SVG or MathML `<style>`, `<script>` or other raw-text-named element at the limit, which keeps no text: its descendants' text is not its own). Pages are unaffected: the deepest of 92 rendered demo-project pages and 316 template files nests 14 levels, and parsing, diffing and serializing them gives byte-identical results before and after. The search for the `dj-root` element no longer walks the raw tree recursively either. The limit is exported as `djust._rust.MAX_VDOM_DEPTH`; the cached-subtree fast paths (a re-rendered component, a loop item) fall back to a full parse when grafting a subtree would pass it. Regression cases in `python/tests/test_vdom_nesting_bound.py` and `crates/djust_vdom/tests/test_nesting_depth_bound.rs`.
