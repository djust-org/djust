- **LogViewer stopped following a stream that trims old lines, and never resumed (#2985).** With `max_lines` set, trimming shrinks the content and the browser may clamp the scroll position; the scroll event for that clamp arrived after more lines had been appended and was read as the reader scrolling up, so from the third burst on the log ended a few pixels short of the newest line. The hook now ignores the position change its own trimming causes. The new Terminal hook has the same follow logic and the same fix.
