- **`{% theme_preset_selector presets="legal,medical,default" %}` and `LIVEVIEW_CONFIG["theme"]["selectable_presets"]` limit the preset list (#3285).** Both take a comma-separated string or a list, in display order; the tag argument wins over the setting, and an unknown name raises (`ValueError` for the tag, `ImproperlyConfigured` for the setting) instead of silently dropping a preset. The setting also limits the presets in `theme_panel` and `theme_switcher` and the `theme_presets` context variable. Regression cases in `python/djust/tests/test_theming_preset_allow_list_3285.py`.
