- **Built-in preset text colours that missed WCAG AA by a small step now meet it (#2885).** 139 `*_foreground`, `link` and `link_hover` tokens across 55 presets move in lightness only, hue and saturation kept, by at most 15 points, never across the light/dark polarity of their surface, and with a 0.05 margin over the minimum; a `link` and its hover move together and the hover keeps its side of the link.
