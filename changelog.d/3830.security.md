- **A POST key of any length now costs linear work to classify against the form floor (#3830).** `_floor_data_key_readable` asks the floor about a `form.data` key and about every `-`-delimited tail of it, and it sliced and hashed every tail in full, so the cost was quadratic in the key's length. A client could POST a key of dashes to any view whose template reads `form.data` on a bound `ModelForm` (or a formset, or `management_form.data`) and hold the worker's GIL: a 32 KB key cost ~0.7 s and a key at Django's 2.5 MB `DATA_UPLOAD_MAX_MEMORY_SIZE` extrapolated to tens of minutes (5,000 keys of 500 dashes: ~11 s). A tail longer than the longest name the floor treats specially (the form's password names, the model's denied / allowlist / opt-out sets, the sensitive model methods and the model's own columns) is now asked in the form of its first `bound + 1` characters, and the keys of a mapping share one set-up (`_DataKeyReader`). The verdict is **unchanged for every key**, including a tail the model arm refuses for starting with `_` or for being absent from an allowlist, which is why the shortcut of skipping long tails was not taken. Counted on the real path (child process, release-independent, pure Python): a 32,000-dash key went from 32,004 questions and 512,016,016 characters to 28 questions and 32,292 characters; a 2,500,000-dash key now takes 0.48 s. The reference function is kept as the oracle in `python/tests/test_floor_data_key_linear_3830.py`, which holds the new one to it over 38,494 generated keys on ten floor shapes, and counts questions and characters, not time.
