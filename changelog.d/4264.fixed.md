- **A loop sorted by a method key on an explicit view renders as Django does, and `{% regroup ... by <method> %}` calls the method on a legacy view too.** `{% for u in users|dictsort:'get_username' %}` (also `dictsortreversed`, a `{% with %}` alias, or a variable naming a method) now renders the empty loop Django renders; before, an explicit view sorted the rows by the called value. Every `dictsort:<variable>` operand takes the JIT on an explicit view, as it does on a legacy one. A `{% regroup users by get_username as g %}` on a legacy view grouped everything under `None`; it now groups by the called value. (#4264)
