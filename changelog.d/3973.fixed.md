- **`djust.Y005` no longer flags an `<svg>` or `role="img"` inside an `aria-hidden="true"` ancestor (#3973).** `aria-hidden="true"` removes the whole subtree from the accessibility tree, so a graphic inside a hidden wrapper needs no name; the check only looked at the element's own attributes. An unhidden unnamed `<svg>`, `aria-hidden="false"` on the wrapper, a sibling after the wrapper closes, a self-closing or void "wrapper" and a wrapper that never closes are still reported. Cases in `TestY005AriaHiddenAncestor3973` of `python/djust/tests/test_accessibility_checks.py`.
