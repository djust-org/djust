- Live navigation now falls back to a full page load when the destination's page shell differs from the page the reader is on (#3036). `dj-navigate`, `auto_navigate`, `live_redirect()` and back/forward used to swap only `dj-root` and keep the previous page's `<head>` stylesheets and outside-root scripts, so a destination with its own stylesheet or `{% block extra_scripts %}` rendered unstyled or dead with no error. Every page now carries a short hash of what its template loads (`<link rel="stylesheet">`, `<script>` outside `dj-root`, `<style>` in `<head>`, head `{% include %}` tags) in `<meta name="djust-page-shell">`; the `mount` reply to a `live_redirect_mount` (WebSocket and SSE) carries the destination's. When they differ the client loads the destination's URL as a normal page; same-shell pages keep the in-place swap. Pages with no fingerprint (no `<head>`, no `dj-root`/`dj-view`, unreadable template) behave as before. Reconciling head assets and scripts in place is deferred. See the navigation guide.
