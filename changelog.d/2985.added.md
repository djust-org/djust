- **SortableList, SortableGrid, JsonViewer and LogViewer now ship the interactions their docstrings describe (#2985).** Each rendered a `dj-hook` that no shipped script answered; `components/static/djust_components/sortable-list.js`, `sortable-grid.js`, `json-viewer.js` and `log-viewer.js` now do, and the catalogue page of each includes its script. Sortable lists and grids reorder by mouse drag or keyboard (Space grabs, arrows move, Enter drops, Escape cancels, each step announced in a live region) and send `{order: [...]}` through `move_event`; their items gain a `data-key` so the server's re-render moves the nodes the client already moved. JsonViewer expands and collapses on click, Enter, Space and the arrow keys, and its Copy button copies the formatted JSON. LogViewer follows the newest line until the reader scrolls up, appends lines pushed to `stream_event`, and honours `max_lines`, `show_line_numbers` and `filter_level` through new `data-*` attributes. The scripts register in `window.DjustHooks` and only when neither hook registry already holds that name, so an app's own hook is never replaced. The other 16 components from the issue are unchanged.
