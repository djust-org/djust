- **The eight component mixins are removed (#4025).** `AccordionMixin`,
  `TabsMixin`, `ModalMixin`, `CollapsibleMixin`, `SheetMixin`, `DropdownMixin`,
  `TooltipMixin` and `CarouselMixin`, with their `*State` classes, are gone from
  `djust.components` and `djust.components.mixins`; they had warned since 1.3.
  Use the matching component in `djust.components.descriptors`. A descriptor
  handles one event, so `open_modal`, `close_modal`, `open_sheet`,
  `close_sheet`, `close_dropdown`, `show_tooltip`, `hide_tooltip`,
  `carousel_prev` and `carousel_next` no longer have a framework handler:
  define them on the view, or point the tag's `*_event` argument at the
  descriptor's event. `ComponentMixin`, `TypedState` and `DataTableMixin` are
  unchanged. Migration table in `docs/API_STABILITY.md`, "Removed in 2.0.0".
