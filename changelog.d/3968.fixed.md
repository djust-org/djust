- **Flash messages are one live region each, not `role="alert"` inside `role="status"` (#3968).** `{% dj_flash %}` rendered its container as `role="status" aria-live="polite"` and the client appended each message as `role="alert"`, nesting live regions, which leaves the announcement (twice, or at the wrong urgency) to each screen reader. The container (both renderers) no longer has a role or `aria-live`; `showFlash` gives `info`, `success` and any other level `role="status"` and `warning` / `error` `role="alert"`. If you wrote your own `#dj-flash-container`, drop its live role too. Not verified with a screen reader. Cases in the `flash live regions (#3968)` block of `tests/js/flash.test.js` and `TestContainerIsNotALiveRegion` of `python/tests/test_flash_tag_handler.py`.
