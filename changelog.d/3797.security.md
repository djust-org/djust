- **A floor field of a form never renders its stored value into HTML, whatever widget the author chose (#3797). BREAKING for a template that relied on an editable floor field echoing its value.** #3796/#3798 refused every way a template could READ a model form's floor-field value back, but `{{ form.password }}`, `{{ form.as_p }}`, `{{ form }}` and `{% for x in form %}{{ x }}{% endfor %}` still printed `value="..."` for a floor field whose widget was a plain `TextInput` (the `password` column, a `DJUST_SENSITIVE_FIELDS` field, a declared `api_token`), the body of a `Textarea`, the `selected` option of a `Select`, the `checked` of a checkbox, and the parts of a `MultiWidget`; `{{ formset.as_p }}`, `as_widget`, `as_field_group`, `BoundField.render()` and the `{name: html}` snapshot of a form kept in LiveView state did the same. A floor field's control is now drawn the way Django draws a field that has no value, by Django's own widget on a copy of the `BoundField` (`value()` and `initial` answer `None`, its own posted keys are removed from a copy of the form, so Django 5's hidden-initial propagation from `form.data` is closed too): no `value=`, an empty `<textarea>`, no `selected`, unchecked, empty `MultiWidget` parts, and a third-party widget needs no cooperation. A field that is not on the floor draws byte-for-byte as Django draws it; the view's own `form["password"].value()` still reads the real value; the stock `django.template` engine is unchanged. djust's component tags (`dj_form`, `combobox`, `rich_select`) draw an EDITABLE floor field without its value too (they kept it until now). **Migration:** an editable control that is drawn empty is posted back empty, so a blank text control clears the column and an unchecked `is_staff` checkbox demotes the user; djust logs it once per field. Declare the form field `disabled=True`, leave it out of the form, or opt the model field out of the floor with `djust_serialize_sensitive_fields`. Kept by their own contract: Django's `ReadOnlyPasswordHashWidget` (exact type; a masked summary) and `PasswordInput(render_value=True)` (the author's explicit echo).
