- **Lock: autobahn 26.7.1 -> 26.10.2 (CVE-2026-116579)** — `autobahn` is reached only through the optional `djust[daphne]` extra (and the dev environment). The lock now constrains it to the fixed release (`>=26.10.1`) and `cbor2` follows it to 6.1.5 on CPython. This constrains `uv.lock`, not the published metadata, so installers of `djust[daphne]` resolve their own latest autobahn.
