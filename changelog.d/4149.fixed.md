- **An explicit-exposure sticky child survives `live_redirect` to another page (#4149).** Under `exposure_policy = "explicit"` the child was re-attached only when its reuse identity matched, and that identity included the request path and the parent's class, so a redirect from `/a/` to `/b/` dropped it and the destination mounted a fresh one; only a redirect back to the same route kept it. The destination's `{% live_render ... sticky=True %}` now re-attaches the preserved instance when the child class and its `state()` declarations, the `sticky_id`, the mount inputs, the session, the user and the tenant match, whatever the route and parent class. Its `state(..., persist="server")` fields are stored under the destination page, and the destination re-runs the child's authorization as before. Unchanged: a bare `dj-sticky-slot` still does not re-attach an explicit child, a child mounted without a session or under a legacy parent is not carried to another page, and SSE keeps no sticky children for either policy.
