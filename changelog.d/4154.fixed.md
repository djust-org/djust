- `{% live_render lazy=True %}` no longer refuses an explicit-exposure child. The child mounts when its slot is filled, as a transient child: the fill reads the session and user from storage again, runs login and `check_permissions` before `mount()` and the object permission after it, and requires the session, user, tenant and parent the page rendered under. A refused fill shows the same `denied access` message in its slot whichever check failed, and the page still renders. The child may not declare persisted fields. (#4154)
