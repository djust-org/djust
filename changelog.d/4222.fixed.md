- **`date`/`time` match Django at DST gaps and folds, for local-mean-time offsets and at the ends of the calendar (#4222).** A wall clock that falls in a DST gap or fold now renders `e`, `T`, `O`, `Z` and `I` empty, as Django does, and `U`/`r` read it with the pre-transition offset (previously `EDT`/`-0400` for a fold and `+0000` for a gap). `O` truncates a seconds offset while `c` and `r` print it (`-04:56:02`), `Y` is zero-padded (`0001`), and `U` truncates toward zero like `int(dt.timestamp())`.
