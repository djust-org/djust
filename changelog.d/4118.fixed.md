- **Uploads: `max_entries` counts finished uploads, a writer's refusal reaches the browser, and the view's `cancel_upload()` settles the browser's upload (#4118).** `max_entries` now counts every entry the slot holds, finished ones too, until they are consumed or cancelled, as documented (a slot with `max_entries=5` used to accept any number of finished uploads); a failed upload does not count. An `UploadError` (or subclass) raised from a writer's `open()`, `write_chunk()` or `close()` is a refusal: it is logged at WARNING without a traceback, and its `message`, one line of at most 200 characters, becomes the upload's error, the `error` in the `djust:upload:progress` detail and the `uploadFile()` rejection message (never `sdk_exc`; any other exception keeps the fixed text and the logged traceback). `UploadMixin.cancel_upload()` now tells the browser at the end of the turn (status `cancelled`, "Upload cancelled by the server."); the client rejects the pending `uploadFile()` promise on a `cancelled` status and stops sending chunks for an upload the server ended.
