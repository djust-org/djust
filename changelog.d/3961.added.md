- **`{% dj_radio_group %}`, and `live_input` radios are a `radiogroup` (#3961).** A lone `{% dj_radio %}` is announced with no group name, required state or description, and `{% live_input "radio" %}` rendered loose `<label><input>` rows. `{% dj_radio_group name="plan" label="Plan" options=plans value=plan event="set_plan" required=True helper="..." error="..." %}` renders a `<fieldset role="radiogroup">` named by its `<legend>`, one input per option with its own `<label for>` (ids `<group>_<index>`), `aria-required`, and the help and error as the group's `aria-describedby` / `aria-invalid`; `field=form.plan` fills it from a `BoundField`. Both engines render the Python tag's markup (it is on the object-argument bridge). `live_input` radios are wrapped in `<div role="radiogroup">` (`legend=` makes it a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`), and the group-level `aria_label` / `aria_labelledby` / `aria_describedby` / `aria_invalid` / `aria_required` go on the group instead of on every radio. Cases in `TestRadioGroup` and `TestLiveInputRadio` of `python/djust/tests/test_component_aria_v1_3_0_19.py`.
