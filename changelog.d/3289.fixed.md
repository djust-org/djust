- **`dj-upload-progress` containers are populated, and `theme_progress` can be wired to an upload (#3289).** The client now attaches the upload ref to each `[dj-upload-progress="<slot>"]` container when an upload starts: an empty container gets a `<progress>` item per file, and a container that already holds a bar (a `.upload-progress-bar`, a `[role=progressbar]` track, or `{% theme_progress %}`) has that bar driven by the upload. `theme_progress` passes extra keyword arguments (`dj_upload_progress`, `dj_hook`, `data_*`, ...) through to its wrapper element.
