- **A template path asks "is this a password field?" by NAME, so a loop over a wide form is no longer quadratic (#3816).** The password-widget floor of #3798 built the form's whole set of password names, scanning every field, for every `BoundField` a template walked: a `{% for f in form %}` over a 100-field form was 100 walked fields x 100 scanned fields per pass, +35-50% of a loop-heavy render (5 fields +5-10%, 30 fields +2-7%). A bound field of a plain form is now decided by `_password_name_held(fields, name)`, which is one dict lookup per way of splitting the name into `<field><suffix>` (so a `MultiWidget`'s `pair_0` / `pair_1` keys are still held), and never builds the form's floor; a `ModelForm`'s floor carries the names as a read-through view of the form's live `fields` instead of an eager set. Same answers (a randomized sweep against the scan covers plain, subclass, drawn-as-password and `MultiWidget` forms with indexed, keyed and empty-keyed parts), with **nothing remembered**: it is not a memo, so a widget swapped on a live form (text -> password -> text), a field added, replaced or removed, or a widget's `input_type` changed in place is followed on the very next step, and no answer can be shared between forms, requests or threads. Release build, loop-heavy templates, interleaved A/B (minimum of 4 rounds each): 100 fields 30.3 -> 17.4 ms (one loop) and 89.2 -> 37.7 ms (three loops), 30 fields 6.5 -> 5.4 ms and 15.9 -> 11.8 ms, 5 fields 1.15 -> 1.13 ms and 2.50 -> 2.40 ms (within noise). A formset's floor, which unions the password names of every form it built, is unchanged (20/100/300 forms showed no measurable difference).

  **Long keys.** `form.data` is keyed by what the client posted, and the by-name check is a lookup per way of splitting the name, so on its own it is quadratic in a key's length (and cubic when `form.data` is filtered, which asks about every `-`-delimited tail of every key): an 8 KB key of dashes pinned a worker for about 30 s. Two guards keep a key's length from deciding how often the form is asked: `_without_floor_keys` reads the names as a SET once per mapping (as before this change, linear in the key), and `_password_name_held` answers a name past 256 characters with one scan of the form. Measured on a bound `UserChangeForm`, `{{ form.data.username }}` with one extra POST key (release): `"-" * 3000` 6.2 ms (1,726 ms without the guards, 6.5 ms before #3816); `"-" * 8000` 20.9 ms (29,576 ms; 20.7 ms); `"a-" * 4000` 12.3 ms (15,038 ms; 12.2 ms).

  Regression cases in `TestAKeyAClientChoseCostsLinearInItsLength` (a count of lookups and scans on 10,000-character plain, dashed and prefixed keys, with exact agreement with the old set), `TestAStepDoesNotScanTheForm` (a `fields` mapping that counts every whole-form read: a step of a 100-field form scans nothing, and the scans of a native loop are the same for 10 fields and for 100), `TestAWidgetSwapOnALiveFormIsFollowed`, `TestNothingIsSharedBetweenForms` and `TestSameAnswerAsTheScan` in `python/tests/test_password_names_by_name_3816.py`.
