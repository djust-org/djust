- **`{% theme_alert %}` gains an `info` variant and a `role` parameter (#3280).** `variant="info"` renders `alert-info` (the `info` token at 10% tint, in `components.css` and the Tailwind output) and `role=` takes `alert`, `status` or `log`. The default is `alert` for `destructive` and `warning` and `status` for the rest, so **upgrade note:** a `default`, `success` or `info` alert now renders `role="status"` (polite) instead of `role="alert"`; pass `role="alert"` to keep the old behaviour. `info` text on its tint joins the contrast matrix (`CONTRAST_PAIRS`, `ThemeTokens.info_tint`); the `legal` dark `info` colour was lightened to pass, and the other presets' 83 failures are documented rows citing #2885. Regression cases in `python/djust/tests/test_theming_alert_info_role_3280.py`.
