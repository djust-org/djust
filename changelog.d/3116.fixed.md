- **A task or thread started inside a turn no longer keeps a disconnected session alive (#3116).**
  The ADR-038 diagnostic owner slots (`djust_diagnostic_owner_slots`) held strong
  references to the consumer and its runtime. Any copy of the context taken
  while a turn was open (an `asyncio` task a view starts, such as a room clock,
  or on Python 3.14+ a thread started from the turn, including the loop's
  default-executor threads) kept the session reachable after the client
  disconnected; on snake-arena with 3.14t, 2–14 consumers stayed alive after
  `gc.collect()`. The slots now hold weak references. An owner that has been
  collected restricts diagnostics, as an unreadable one already did.
