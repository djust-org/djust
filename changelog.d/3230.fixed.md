It also set no background, so a `<button class="btn">` kept the browser's light `buttonface` under the light dark-mode text: in headless Chrome, 59 of the other design systems rendered it at a 1.10:1 contrast ratio in dark mode. The plain `.btn` rule now sits in `@layer components`, the layer the theme uses, and a zero-specificity `:where(.btn)` gives it `hsl(var(--muted))`, so a design system's own `.btn` background still wins.
