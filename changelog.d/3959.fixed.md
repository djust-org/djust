- **Multi-value form fields lose values: `dj-submit` kept only the last `FormData` entry, `dj-change` on a checkbox sent its checked state (#3959).** A checkbox group (two or more same-named checkboxes in one form), a `<select multiple>` and any repeated name now reach a `dj-submit` handler as a list in document order, the way Django's `QueryDict.getlist` sees a native POST; a group or `<select multiple>` is a list even with one value selected or none (`[]`), so unticking everything replaces the server's earlier list instead of leaving it stale. `dj-change` on one box of a group sends the list of checked values, and on a `<select multiple>` every selected option (it sent the box's boolean, and the first option), so `FormMixin.validate_field` validates a `MultipleChoiceField`. Reconnect form recovery replays a group as one list-valued `dj-change`. A lone checkbox is unchanged. Under the strict policy declare `list[str]` (or `Any`).
