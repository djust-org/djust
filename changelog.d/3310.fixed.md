  its nonce to `theme.js` (`window.__djust_theme_nonce`, set by the already-nonced anti-flash script) and gives its own
  theme `<style>` / `<link>` the `id="djust-theme-css"` that `setPresetWithoutReload` looks up, so the switch updates the
  nonced element in place; any `<style>` theme.js does have to create carries the nonce. The inline `onload` on the
  deferred stylesheet is unchanged (use `link_css=True` or `critical_css: False` under a strict policy). 12 regression
  cases in `python/djust/tests/test_theming_live_switch_nonce_3310.py`, 5 in
  `tests/js/theming_live_switch_csp_nonce_3310.test.js`.
