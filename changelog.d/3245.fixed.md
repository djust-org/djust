- **A second `mount` or `mount_batch` frame on a mounted WebSocket now tears the replaced view down first (#3245).** The stock client sends these frames on a live socket: lazy hydration mounts each `dj-lazy` view with its own `mount` frame (and falls back to per-view mounts when `mount_batch` is refused), or one `mount_batch`. The replaced view was dropped with no teardown: its channel-layer groups kept the socket, so a push aimed at it was handled by the new view and the membership outlived the disconnect; its waiters were left for the garbage collector; its children were never unregistered. It now gets the `live_redirect` teardown: it leaves its view, presence, presence-scope, db_notify and scoped-push groups, its tick task and deferred pushes are cancelled, and the view is released (`release_root_view`, #3244). A `mount_batch`'s own views are siblings, not replacements of one another: each keeps its groups while the socket is open, and a later replacement or the disconnect tears them all down (the view group of every batch view but the last used to leak past the disconnect). New cases in `python/djust/tests/test_view_replacement_teardown_3244_3245.py`.
