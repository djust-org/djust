- **Built-in preset labels that failed WCAG AA on their fill now meet it; no fill, background or border token moves (#2885).** For 57 presets, 374 `*_foreground`, `link` and `link_hover` tokens move in lightness only (hue and saturation kept) to the nearest lightness that reads at 4.5:1 on their fill and does not read worse where else the label is painted: label text on solid fills flips between light and dark ink for the 337 of them that cross sides (white on a bright button becomes dark ink), and the other 37 are same-side moves. A solid button's hover fill (the fill at 75-90% alpha over the page or a card) and the `--code` surface of syntax comments are guarded: a move that cannot keep them is held (tailwind and everforest light `muted_foreground`). A link and its hover move together and keep their order. Matrix failures drop from 540 to 158 and 382 exemptions go. The generated CSS of all 68 presets differs only in the label and link variables, never in a fill, border or dot variable. `--muted-foreground` is also drawn as dots, switch tracks, scrollbar thumbs and spinners; its 8 larger moves make those more contrasting against the page, card and muted surfaces (no painted use gets weaker). Rules that read a label on a surface the contrast matrix does not measure were fixed: the tooltip bubble pairs `--background` with its `--foreground` fill, the rich-select status variants read the derived `*-text` colours of their wash, and the data-card filter chip no longer swaps `--accent` under its active label on hover. A test scans every rule that sets `color` from a `*-foreground` token (and every state rule of its element family) and requires the background to be that fill. Exempt on purpose, with their reasons recorded: the tokens whose source documents a brand hex (monokai and stripe `link`, github and stripe muted text), which keep that hex, the two held code-surface tokens, and the 131 `input` borders (non-text UI components; 3:1 not applied to the shipped presets, revisit on request; no border changes). Still open: `accent_foreground`, which `.status-badge-accent` paints as a background. `scripts/fix_theme_text_contrast.py --include-substantial` applies and checks this.
