- **Test files no longer end a wait for a frame that arrives with a 2-3 s window; the #3709 ratchet counts those waits per file (#3726).** `test_live_auth_outside_declarations.py`, `test_live_auth_event_holders_and_user_checks.py`, `test_model_form_route_change_3125.py` and `test_mount_object_permission_after_params.py` read frames until a `mount`, a close or a navigation answer that DOES arrive in the positive case, with `receive_output(timeout=2)` / `receive_json_from(timeout=3)` inside a `try: ... except: pass`, and then asserted it had arrived. A runner that stalled past that window failed 68 tests (a 3.5 s delay on every outgoing frame reproduces it). They now wait with `djust.tests._ws_frames.receive_until` (a 10 s deadline of its own, not the 30 s default) and return the decoded frames. `test_security_view_import_failclosed.py` and `test_sync_turn_wrapper_more_hops_3637.py` wait for the `error` frame that refuses the mount before they assert on the absence of a `mount` or on the state (the 3637 one asserted "no mount" against `[]` under a stall); `test_recovery_version_staleness_1817.py` passes 30 instead of 2 / 3 to its `_recv`; and `test_anonymous_event_after_refused_mount_does_not_run` now sends its event whatever the mount answered (it never did). `test_communicator_timeouts_ratchet_3709.py` no longer exempts a `try` whose handlers only `pass` / `break` / `continue`, and counts per file a communicator wait whose timeout reads as a number under 10 s: a literal, arithmetic on literals, a module constant, `**{"timeout": 2}`, in a keyword or a positional slot, wrapped in `asyncio.wait_for` / `asyncio.timeout` or not, and a call to a forwarding helper with a short keyword or positional timeout. What it cannot read as a number (a parameter, a call) and bounds on things that are not frame waits stay in `UNCOVERED_BASELINE` as totals that cannot name the file. Genuine absence windows sit in `BASELINE` with a reason in `BASELINE_REASONS`; the last line of a `pytest.raises` block is still exempt. #3709 stays open for the variable-timeout and `wait_for` classes.
