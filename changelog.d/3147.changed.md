- **The vendored ttyd terminal now ships xterm 6 (#3147).** `@xterm/xterm` 5.5.0 to 6.0.0 and `@xterm/addon-fit` 0.10.0 to 0.11.0. `ttyd_terminal.js` needed no change: it only uses `Terminal({rows, cols, theme, convertEol})`, `loadAddon`, `open`, `write`, `onData`, `rows`/`cols`, `FitAddon.fit` and `dispose`, all unchanged in v6. xterm 6 draws its own scrollbar and removes the long-deprecated `windowsMode`, `fastScrollModifier` and the flat `overviewRulerWidth` option; djust passes none of them, but a project that copied the hook and sets one needs to adapt. The bundle, its SRI hashes, `djust_assets.json` and `djust.cdx.json` are regenerated with `make vendor`, and a vitest test now loads the committed bundle through the hook's own `import()`.
