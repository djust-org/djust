- **The template engine's date filters and timezone conversion moved from `chrono` + `chrono-tz` to `jiff` (#4222).** Both crates are marked unmaintained by RustSec (RUSTSEC-2026-0543, RUSTSEC-2026-0555); `Cargo.lock` no longer carries either, and their `osv-scanner.toml` exceptions are removed. The IANA tz database is bundled into the extension (no dependency on the host's `/usr/share/zoneinfo`). Rendered output is unchanged: the RFC 3339 grammar, zone-name lookup (still case-sensitive) and offset printing keep their previous behaviour, and a Django differential over DST gaps and folds, far-future dates and negative offsets pins it.
