- **Child-render `{% include %}` guard** (#3931): a patch of `IncludeNode.render` made by someone else (a replacement, or a `functools.wraps` wrapper of Django's own put in place before the first scope) no longer switches the guard off for the process; the guard is put over it and delegates to it. A replacement whose `__wrapped__` raises no longer makes every scope entry raise, the guard is found under any `functools.wraps` chain depth, and the "guard is not in place" WARNING is gone. The census of Django-engine renders also sees `getattr(t, "render")`, an unbound `Template.render(t, c)`, a template held on `self`, and walrus, tuple-unpack, annotated, loop and `with` bindings.
