- **`dj-model` follow-ups (#3355).** The debounce timer of a field is now keyed by
  the view the element lives in as well as the field name, so two lazy views
  binding the same debounced field no longer drop one view's update. A
  `contenteditable` element now sends its text as `value` (with `.lazy`, when it
  loses focus), and `dj-model.debounce-N` is found on controls and `contenteditable`
  elements. The scan that runs after every patch skips an unchanged bound element
  on its recorded attribute instead of walking its attributes: in Chromium a
  re-scan of 2,000 bound inputs among 4,000 inputs and 2,000 other elements fell
  from about 5.0 ms to 1.1 ms (2,000 bound inputs alone: 3.3 ms to 0.6 ms).
